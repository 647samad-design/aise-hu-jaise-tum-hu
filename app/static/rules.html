Contest Rules & Fair Play

Last updated: 1 November 2026

In short: pay a small entry fee, get game attempts, play Whack-a-Mole, climb the Top 20. When the timer ends, the winner is decided as shown on the contest page.

1. How to join

  1. Sign in with Google and complete your registration (name, mobile number, city; 18+).
  2. Pick the live contest and pay the entry fee. After we verify your payment you receive the attempts shown on the contest page.
  3. Play. Your best score across all your attempts counts.

2. The games

Each contest uses one game, shown on the contest page. You have 20 seconds per attempt. Every attempt has its own random sequence, so playing well, not memorising, is what matters. Your score is calculated on our server from your taps, not on your phone.

🐹 Whack-a-Mole

Moles pop out of 12 holes and the game speeds up as time passes.

EventPoints
🐹 Whack a mole+1
🌟 Whack a golden mole+3
💣 Hit a bomb−3
Tap an empty hole−1

🍎 Fruit Catch

Objects fall from the top of the screen and later start to sway. Tap an object to catch it while it is on screen.

EventPoints
🍎 Catch a fruit+1
💎 Catch a gem (falls faster)+3
💣 Tap a bomb−3
Tap on nothing−1

Your final score for an attempt is never below 0.

3. Leaderboard

  • The leaderboard shows the 20 highest best-scores. If two players have the same score, the one who reached it first ranks higher.
  • The leaderboard updates live. Players can move in and out of the Top 20 until the contest ends.

4. Choosing the winner

When the countdown ends, the Top 20 is locked. Then the result is determined as described on the contest page.

Every contest publishes a fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) of a secret key before it starts. After the draw we reveal the key, the list of finalists and the calculation, so anyone can check that nothing was changed. You can see the proof in the app next to every past winner.

5. Fair play and disqualification

Bots, scripts, auto-clickers, multiple accounts, shared accounts, tampered apps or any attempt to cheat leads to removal of scores and possible suspension. Scores that look suspicious are held and reviewed by our team before they can win. See the Terms.

6. Prize delivery

  • We contact the winner using the phone number given at registration.
  • The winner must pass identity and age verification.
  • We publish prize hand-over proof (such as photos or video) for transparency, with the winner’s consent where required.
