Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

JeetoPrizes respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: name, email and profile name from your Google sign-in; the name, mobile number and city you give at registration.
  • Gameplay data: scores, tap timings, attempts and leaderboard position (used for fair play checks).
  • Payment data: the method, transaction ID and amount you submit. We do not see your wallet PIN or card details.
  • Technical data: device and browser type, IP address and basic logs, used for security and to prevent multiple or fraudulent accounts.
  • Communications: messages you send to our support.

2. How we use it

  • To run the contests, show leaderboards and declare winners.
  • To verify payments and send you service notifications.
  • To contact winners by phone, WhatsApp or email and deliver prizes.
  • To detect cheating, fraud and abuse, and to keep the service secure.
  • To meet legal obligations.

3. What other players see

Your display name and score appear on the leaderboard and, if you win, your name may be announced. We never show your phone number or email to other players.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us run the service (for example hosting and payment providers), and with authorities when the law requires it.

5. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active and for as long as needed for contest records, fraud prevention, accounting and legal reasons. You can ask us to delete your account; some records may need to be kept by law.

6. Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent for optional notifications (you can also switch off browser notifications in your device settings). Contact us using the details below.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, such as encrypted connections and restricted admin access. No online service can be completely risk-free.

8. Children

The service is only for people aged 18 and over. If we learn that someone under 18 registered, we will remove the account.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will show the date above.

10. Contact

Email: our support team · WhatsApp support
